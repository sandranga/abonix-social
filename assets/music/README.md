# Music for reels

Put music tracks here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac` or `.wav`). Each reel uses one,
rotating so consecutive reels differ, trimmed to the video with a short fade
in and out. With no tracks here, reels get a silent audio track.

Only tracks you are licensed to use on social media, for a business account,
e.g. from Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music, free for commercial use) or a
paid library. Instagram's trending sounds can't be added: Meta's API doesn't
give access to its music library. Keep files small (a 1-2 minute mp3 is
2-4 MB) since they're stored in git.
